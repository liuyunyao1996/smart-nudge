## 2. Sun Life survey finds demand for integrated support

**Attention:** MEDIUM | INFORMATIONAL

Company-published Sun Life research across eight North American and Asian markets found that 65% of adults wanted support treating health, finances and protection as interconnected. However, only 33% reported having a complete plan for unexpected illness or accident costs, and 31% had a complete plan for family security. The Asian coverage comprised Hong Kong, the Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia, Malaysia and Singapore.

- Sixty-one percent of respondents believed their health affected their financial position.
- Fifty-nine percent believed their financial position affected their health.
- The findings indicate a gap between demand for holistic support and respondents’ reported preparedness for health shocks and family-protection needs.

**Potential impact:** The findings support the potential relevance of propositions that connect health services, financial planning and protection, while identifying sizable preparedness gaps across several markets shared by Sun Life and AIA.

## 3. FSB consults on modernising regulatory and supervisory frameworks

**Attention:** MEDIUM | CONSULTATION

**Potential impact:** The final principles could shape the direction of supervisory reform across multiple AIA markets, potentially affecting supervisory methods, regulatory coherence and the treatment of technology-enabled products and emerging vulnerabilities.
