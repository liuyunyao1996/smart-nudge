## 2. AXA report ranks AI and big-data risks first

**Attention:** MEDIUM | TECHNOLOGY / OPERATIONAL RISK

AXA’s company-issued 13th Future Risks Report, produced with Ipsos and published on 12 October 2026, ranked AI and big-data risks first among surveyed experts for the first time. The category rose from fourth place in 2025 and overtook climate change. The report also connected ageing, lower birth rates, longevity and changing family structures with evolving insurance needs across health, dependency protection, savings, retirement and wealth transfer.

- Among experts who ranked AI among their leading risks, 54% regarded it as rapidly emerging.
- Only 9% of those experts considered public authorities well prepared for AI-related risks.
- The demographic findings span multiple insurance propositions and long-term financial needs rather than a single product segment.

**Potential impact:** The findings indicate potential growth in customer and institutional concern about AI-related exposures while reinforcing demand drivers associated with ageing and longevity. For the wider insurance sector, these trends could influence product design, underwriting considerations, risk communication and long-term savings propositions.
